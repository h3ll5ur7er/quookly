A Story About My Aunt

My aunt kept a kitchen that smelled of rain. She never wrote anything down, and when she died we found nothing at all in her drawers but string and a single photograph of a dog nobody could identify. I have thought about that dog for thirty years, and I am no closer to knowing whose it was, or why she kept it where she kept the string.

There is no recipe here. There was never going to be one. Some things are only ever about the person who made them, and this is one of those things.
