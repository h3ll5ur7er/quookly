What Quookly does

☰

Bring a recipe in from anywhere

Paste a link. Quookly reads the page — or asks a model you host yourself — and keeps the recipe. The eleven paragraphs about somebody's holiday stay behind.

▦

Cook what you already have

Tell it what is in the cupboard and it will say what is worth cooking, starting with whatever is about to go off. Waste is mostly a memory problem.

▤

Plan a week, shop once

Meals are sized to the appetites at the table, not the head count. The shopping list is what your kitchen has not got — and you tick it off in the shop.

✓

Made for the people you cook for

Record an allergy once. Every recipe is judged against it, and a recipe nobody has classified says so rather than saying it is safe.

It runs on your machine

Quookly is self-hosted. Your recipes, your household's allergies and what you ate last Tuesday stay on hardware you control. If it talks to a model at all, it talks to the one you point it at.
